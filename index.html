Bramble’s Summit

Baron Tusker has kidnapped Professor Goat and carried him to a castle in the clouds. Guide Bramble from the forest floor all the way up to the sky! Each world has two rides (a hot air balloon, a zip line, a mine cart and more) that only take you aboard if you solve a 4th grade math challenge, and the castle door needs one more. The math gets trickier the higher you climb. Get one wrong and you start the world over. You’ll never see the same question twice.

Move
← → or A / D
Jump
Space, ↑ or W (hold for a higher jump)
Checkpoint rides
Walk up to a ride with a ? badge to get your math question. Answer correctly to ride to the next part of the world. If you fall, you start again at your last ride
Snot rockets
Some bricks hide a sneezeweed. Bump them from below, then chase it down before it rolls into a pit or off the screen. Fire with F or X
Bonus round
After each world, answer as many math questions as you can in 90 seconds. Each correct answer earns a slot machine spin for lives, snot rockets or coins
Spikes
Silver spikes with red tips hurt! Some pop up and down out of yellow-striped plates. Wait for them to drop, then run across
Stomp
Land on enemies from above. Stomp a shellback, then kick its shell into other enemies
Castles
Cross the castle’s lava halls (watch out for flame jets!), then beat the boss. Stomp it or sneeze on it to free whoever is in the cage
The sky
In the last world some clouds drift side to side, some ride up and down, and the purple-striped ones vanish soon after you land on them
Pause
Esc or the Menu button pauses the game and lets you return to this main menu
